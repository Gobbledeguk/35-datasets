Age of Mortals (Sovereign Press, 2003) — importable pack

Import: Settings > Source Books > Import Homebrew (.zip), and pick Age_of_Mortals.zip.
The PDF has no text layer, so every number was read on the page images. Rules text is condensed from the book,
numbers exact.

What it carries:
  - 2 races: the Tarmak (pp.10-11) and the half-kender (pp.21-23).
  - The Mariner as this book prints it (pp.24-27), named "Mariner (Age of Mortals)". Legends of the Twins revises
    it, and that is the Mariner the Races of Ansalon options name; import that pack for the current class.
  - 12 prestige classes: academy sorcerer, Citadel mystic, kender nightstalker, Legion mystic, Legion scout, Legion
    sorcerer, master ambassador, nomad shaman, rogue knight, Solamnic auxiliary mage, spellfilch, war mage.
  - 19 feats (pp.50-51, 56), the three Leech Item feats among them.
  - 2 mystic domains, Channeling and Sensitivity (p.66).
  - 32 spells (pp.66-77), call undead I to IX among them.
  - 2 weapons and the Tarmak war paint, 13 magic items and artifacts (pp.77-81 and the sidebars on pp.123, 151, 166
    and 198), and the Device of Time Journeying.
  - 2 templates: bound spirit and ogre titan (pp.200-205).

Shared names: the 16 feats Legends of the Twins reprints, the cutlass and the Device of Time Journeying carry
"(Age of Mortals)", so this pack and that one can both be on without one replacing the other. Legends of the Twins
(p.12) says its versions are the current ones.

Where the book contradicts itself (both readings are in the text):
  - Academy sorcerer, Cooperative Study (p.29): Table 1-4 gives +2/+4/+6, the text +3/+5/+7.
  - Spellfilch spellcasting (p.47): Table 1-19 advances it at odd levels, the text at even. The pack follows the table.
  - Kender nightstalker (p.34): Table 1-8's footnote says Charisma, every other line Wisdom. The pack uses Wisdom.
  - Spear of divine wrath (p.75) names "spear of divine might" in its neutral clause.

Not applied by the app (the text says them; nothing adds them to the sheet):
  - Bonus feats come as a free feat choice, not limited to the class's list.
  - The kender nightstalker's caster level is half its class level; the app uses the full level.
  - Conditional numbers (desperate strike, reputation, favored embassy and so on) stay in each feature's text.

Not included (not character options): the variant rule on magic drain over the Fifth Age (p.59) and undead magic
drain; the Additional Options sidebar letting a wizard take Wizard of High Sorcery unspecialized (p.65: no Enhanced
Specialization at 1st level); Table 2-3, Krynn's spiritual weapons by deity (p.76: as the PHB spell, with each god's
weapon); advanced, sea and shadow dragons, skull totems and the Dragon Overlords (pp.206-221); NPC stat blocks.
