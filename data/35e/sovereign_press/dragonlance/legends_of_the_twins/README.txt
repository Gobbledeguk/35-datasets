Legends of the Twins (Sovereign Press, 2007) — importable pack

Import: Settings > Source Books > Import Homebrew (.zip), and pick Legends_of_the_Twins.zip.
Every number was read on the book's page. The rules text is condensed from the book, numbers exact.

What it carries:
  - Mariner, the base class (pp.13-16): the revised version this book says replaces Age of Mortals'.
  - Knight of the Divine Hammer, the prestige class (pp.16-18).
  - 35 character traits (pp.7-11): the variant lets a character take up to two.
  - 18 feats (pp.12-13).
  - 15 spells: the River of Time spells (pp.24-27) and Tear of Veil's Parting (p.156).
  - The cutlass (p.16), 11 magic items (pp.27-29, plus the dagger of righteous vengeance, p.120) and the
    three Artifacts of Time (pp.29-31).

What it does not do, and why:
  - The Mariner's bonus feats: the app opens a free feat choice at 1st, 5th, 10th, 15th and 20th level; it does
    not hold you to the book's list (the feature's text names it).
  - Conditional numbers (Back-to-Back's dodge bonus, Dirty Strike's damage, a trait's bonus against fear) are in
    each feature's text, not added to the sheet. Seamanship's bonus is added: +1 to +5 on Balance, Climb and
    Profession (sailor).
  - The Knight of the Divine Hammer needs Honor-Bound, a Dragonlance Campaign Setting feat: turn that book on.
  - The book's NPC stat blocks (Chapters 3-5) are not character options and are not included.
  - Prices are in steel pieces, which the book trades 1 for 1 with gold pieces.
