Dragon Magazine Compendium - PCGen conversion notes (races section, pp. 3-74)

Install: copy this folder to data/35e/paizo/ in a bahamutdragon/pcgen data tree.
Game mode: Bahamut35e. Requires the core books.

Conditional files (load only when the other book is selected):
  dmc_races_xph.lst / dmc_abilities_xph.lst  -> Expanded Psionics Handbook (12 Athasian races)
  dmc_races_ca.lst                           -> Complete Arcane (Umbragen; favored class Warlock)
  dmc_races_cadv.lst                         -> Complete Adventurer (Insectare; favored class Spellthief)

Name clashes with races already in the repo use a "(DMC)" key and OUTPUTNAME:
  Bozak, Tortle, Grippli, Azerblood, Mul. Athasian Pterran/Thri-Kreen use an "Athasian" prefix.

Partly or wholly descriptive (DESC only, apply by hand):
  - Situational save bonuses (para-genasi, azerblood, khepri, modron, etc.)
  - Bozak 4th-level sorcerer casting; giff firearms proficiency; gythka/chatkcha familiarity
  - Ghost elf slip binding / ethereal shift; glimmerfolk nimli spells; worghest wolf form
  - Athasian human's chosen inborn powers; aarakocra Craft (any one) bonus
  - Exiled modron favored class = first class taken (approximated with HIGHESTLEVELCLASS)
  - Celadrin Melodious Voice is applied to all Diplomacy
  - Saurian shifter traits apply while the "Saurian Shifting" temporary bonus is active
  - Remade: apply "Remade", then one "Remade: <enhancement>" template per enhancement and an
    equal number of "Remade: <defect>" templates. Unnatural Weapon assumes a Medium creature.

Feats, Flaws & Styles (compendium pp. 1058-1176)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
dmc_feats.lst         550 feats (incl. 13 fighting-style mastery feats and 4 [Style] feats), CATEGORY:FEAT
dmc_flaws.lst          80 flaws, built on Unearthed Arcana's flaw rules (TYPE:Flaws, +1 feat each);
                          loads only when "Unearthed Arcana (Complete)" is selected
dmc_spelltemplates.lst  7 spell templates (Bloodleach, Champion, ...) granted by their ceremonial feats

- Feat text (flavor, Benefit, Normal, Special, suggested class/race) is carried over from the compendium;
  DESC ends with the original Dragon Magazine issue and article in [brackets].
- Prerequisites were converted to PRE tags where possible (stats, BAB, skill ranks, level, class level,
  race, alignment, deity, spellcasting, feats from the core books or this set, "any X feat").
  Anything else (class features, regions, feats from other books, ...) is listed in PRETEXT,
  which PCGen shows but does not enforce.
- Simple, unconditional bonuses were added automatically (e.g. "+2 bonus on Bluff and Intimidate checks",
  flaw penalties). Conditional or choice-based effects are text only.
- Feats whose name already exists elsewhere in the repo got a "(DMC)" KEY (e.g. Bloodthirsty, Agile Tyrant);
  the two "Horse Nomad" feats are split into "Horse Nomad" (ancestor) and "Horse Nomad (Regional)".
- Typos in the source were kept in the text; one obvious prerequisite typo (Tumble 55 ranks -> 5) was fixed.
- The fighting-style overviews (lists of recommended feats) and the summary tables (pp. 1177-1197) were not
  converted, since they repeat information already on the feats.

Arms and Equipment (compendium pp. 1198-1270)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  and the Table 5-4 "power components for non-magical items" (they are just masterwork versions of PHB items).
- A few descriptions pick up an image caption from the page (e.g. "A warhammer forged from Byeshk").

Magic Items (compendium pp. 1420-1595)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
dmc_magic_weapons.lst   65 specific magic weapons, built on their base weapon (PHB or this set)
dmc_magic_armor.lst     21 specific magic armors and shields, built on their base armor/shield
dmc_magic_items.lst    561 other items: wondrous items by body slot (amulets, cloaks, rings, rods, staffs,
                           wands, tools, books, instruments...), grafts, magic face paints, warforged items,
                           vehicles and vehicle augments, plus specific weapons/armor with no clear base item
dmc_magic_eqmods.lst    28 weapon/armor special properties (PLUS:n or flat gp cost) and magic poison qualities

- Specific weapons/armor: the base item is detected from the text ("This +2 banded mail..."); the item copies
  the base item's stats, uses the listed market price as its cost, and gets its enhancement bonus as a
  BONUS:WEAPON / BONUS:COMBAT. Other special abilities (keen, flaming, etc.) are text only.
- SPROP shows aura, caster level, body slot, activation and item level; DESC holds the description,
  rules text, prerequisites, cost to create, lore, and the Dragon/Dungeon issue.
- Artifacts and relics are typed Artifact (Minor/Major) or Relic; priceless items have COST:0.
- Body slots map to the usual PCGen item types (Throat=Amulet, Shoulders=Cape, Face=Eyegear, Body=Robe,
  Torso=Shirt, Arms=Bracer, Hands=Glove, Waist=Belt, Feet=Boot). Item effects are text only; no automatic bonuses.
- Not converted: the "Magic Item Sets" chapter (Heroes of Might and Magic artifacts described in tables) and the
  price/level/source index appendices.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Validation (sanity check)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The whole set was loaded in PCGen 6.08.00 RC10 with the repo's Bahamut35e game mode plus its Core Books,
Expanded Psionics Handbook, Tome of Magic, Spell Compendium and Unearthed Arcana data, and test characters
were exported headlessly. Fixes made as a result:
- The PCC listed the equipment-modifier files as EQMOD: instead of EQUIPMOD:, so they were not loaded. Fixed.
- About 30 descriptions with unbalanced brackets (from the PDF text) were rejected; all text fields are now balanced.
- ADD:ABILITY on Complacent Human / Athasian Human used the AUTOMATIC nature, which ADD does not allow; now VIRTUAL.
- KEYs containing commas or colons (power components, some spells and modifiers) were renamed and every reference
  updated, since PCGen prohibits those characters in keys.
- Spell text that had spilled into Spell Resistance / Duration / Saving Throw is moved back into the description;
  one spell with an empty description is fixed.
- Warduke's Helm (an artifact helm) is a wondrous item, no longer a longsword.
After these fixes the DMC files produce no load errors (the remaining errors in the log come from the repo's own
files, which target a newer PCGen than 6.08). All 14 new base classes, every race, the Remade template and all 550
feats were placed on test characters and exported. Please still test in the PCGen version you use.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Spelling / OCR clean-up of descriptions
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A conservative clean-up pass is applied to all DESC, BENEFIT and SPROP text (and spell stat lines):
- words split by line-break hyphenation are rejoined ("dif- ficult" -> "difficult", "con- structed" ->
  "constructed"); real compounds keep their hyphen ("full- round" -> "full-round", "5th- level" -> "5th-level");
- OCR letter "o" for zero in numbers is fixed ("5,ooo gp" -> "5,000 gp", "3o-foot" -> "30-foot", "5o%" -> "50%");
- about 70 specific misspellings and run-together words are corrected (effefective, immediatley, canot, misslie,
  amnorsmiths, bull'strength, dominateperson, limitedwish, lggwilv -> Iggwilv, ...);
- OCR "can he used" -> "can be used", and a few stray page headers are removed.
In total about 430 corrections. Game terms (warforged, mithral, hexblade, ...) and proper names are left alone, and
nothing was changed where the right word wasn't certain. Real-word OCR errors (a wrong but valid word) can't be
found automatically and may remain.

Advanced feat syntax (expert feedback)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Leadership feats now use the repo's Leadership support variables (LeadershipScore, LeadershipMaxCohortLvl,
FollowersLVL1-6 / FollowersLVLnCount) instead of text:
- "Leadership score N higher than your level" -> PREVARGTEQ:LeadershipScore,TL+N
  (Assemble the Horde, Class Champion, Close Cohort, Eye for Talent, Fanatical Devotion)
- Assemble the Horde: +1 score, plus extra 1st-level followers equal to all higher-level followers.
- Class Champion: +1 score, plus 2 followers of the highest level available.
- Close Cohort: cohort may be up to your level -1 (instead of -2), still limited by the score table.
- Eye for Talent, Fanatical Devotion +1; Improved Leadership, Natural Leader +2; Noble Born +2 only if you
  have Leadership. Noble Born and Recognized Elder also add their class skills, and Recognized Elder its
  +1 bonus on Diplomacy and Knowledge (history).
Verified in PCGen 6.08 on a 10th-level fighter (Cha 18): score 17 and 35/3/1/1 followers with Assemble the
Horde; 3 4th-level followers and a 9th-level cohort cap with Class Champion + Close Cohort.

Other text prerequisites now use the repo's class-feature abilities and variables where they exist:
familiar, rage/frenzy, bardic music, turn undead, turn or rebuke undead, wild shape, flurry of blows, animal
companion, special mount, evasion, smite (PREABILITY by ability TYPE); sneak attack +Nd6 (SneakAttackDice),
favored enemy (FavoredEnemyCount), arcane/divine/any caster level (Caster_Level_Highest__Arcane/__Divine/
Caster_Level_Highest), and Hit Dice (PREHD). PRETEXT is still used for things the repo doesn't track
(e.g. curse ability, ki power, mind blade, regions, tribe membership). Fixed source typo "228 HD" -> "28 HD".
Feat effects that are situational (once per day, against fear, cohort of a given class, ...) stay as text.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Advanced feat syntax, part 2 (repo techniques, done in 5 checked chunks)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Repeatable feats and metamagic
   - 20 feats that may be taken more than once now have MULT:YES with STACK:YES + CHOOSE:NOCHOICE (effects stack:
     Extra Curse, Extra Remove Disease) or STACK:NO + a CHOOSE (each copy applies to something different: favored
     enemy for Favored Dodge/Intimidate the Enemy/No Threat to Me/Tactical Advantage/Trophy Hunter, spellcasting
     class, domain, melee weapon, metamagic feat, creature type, or a typed-in choice).
   - Metamagic slot costs: ADDSPELLLEVEL 1 (Forceful, Slimy), 2 (Radiant), 0 (Alternative Source).
     Relicguard Spell has no slot cost in the compendium, so none is set.
2. Choice feats (17): CHOOSE with %LIST bonuses in the repo's style, e.g. Flexible Mind (2 skills -> class skills
   +1), Dwarflore/Mercantile Background/Cultured Courtier/Born Under a Setting Sun (chosen skill bonuses),
   Polyglot (chosen language), Diversified Casting/Psiotheurgist (school), Divine Sorcery (domain), Innate Magic
   (0-level spell). PCGen 6.08 rejects CHOOSE without MULT:YES, so these use MULT:YES + STACK:NO like the repo's
   Spell Focus/Weapon Focus.
3. Senses, speed, HP, proficiencies, spell DC, turning, flat bonuses: DarkvisionRange/HasLowlightVision
   (Deepseer, Orc Blooded, Eyes of the Mage, Superior Vision), MOVEADD (Fire Feet, Initiate of Fharlanghn),
   HP (Hardy Soul), AUTO:WEAPONPROF (Horse Nomad - the light lance isn't in the core data), BONUS:DC|DESCRIPTOR
   (Earth Focus), TurnCheckUndead/TurnDamagePlusUndead (Initiate of Pelor), AC/ranged attack (Temerad Mastery II,
   Eyes of the Mage), class skills (Initiate of Ehlonna).
4. Situational bonuses: 64 feats and flaws have TEMPBONUS toggles (with TEMPDESC saying when to switch them on),
   shown on PCGen's Temporary Bonus tab. Bonuses that help allies use ANYPC (Aura of Bravery, Commander, Gang
   Tactics, Presence of the Mage...). Each was reviewed by hand; effects on enemies, penalty reductions,
   cumulative or trade-off bonuses stay as text. Permanent bonuses found during the review were added as normal
   bonuses (Hard to Fool, Raider's Spirit, Breath Control, Mind Over Body for arcane casters).
5. Bloodline feats: 13 bloodlines add their 9 bonus spells known to the sorcerer (SPELLKNOWN:CLASS|Sorcerer=n),
   each only once you can cast spells of that level. Draconic Bloodline's 3rd-level "protection from elements"
   (a 3.0 spell) stays as text. Fixed Anarchic Bloodline's merged prerequisite (familiar is now checked).
Verified in PCGen 6.08 after each chunk (load with no DMC errors + test characters): feats taken twice show
"(2x)", choices show on the sheet and apply their bonuses, toggles change nothing until switched on and then
apply (+2 dodge AC, +10 ft. speed, +4 saves, +2 attack), and a 9th-level sorcerer with Air Bloodline gains
exactly the 1st-4th level bloodline spells.

Spell format aligned with the repo's Player's Handbook (ph_spells.lst / ph_spells_class.lst)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Token order follows the PHB: TYPE, SCHOOL, SUBSCHOOL, DESCRIPTOR, COMPS, CASTTIME, RANGE, ITEM, TARGETAREA,
  DURATION, SAVEINFO, SPELLRES, SOURCEPAGE (then DESC).
- Class and domain lists moved out of the spell lines into dmc_spells_class.lst as "<spell>.MOD  CLASSES:..
  DOMAINS:.." lines, like ph_spells_class.lst (loaded after dmc_spells.lst in the PCC).
- RANGE uses the PHB categories: "Close", "Medium", "Long" (formula text dropped), "Touch", "Personal", or a fixed
  distance.
- DURATION uses the PHB formulas: "1 round/level" -> "(CASTERLEVEL) rounds", "10 min./level" ->
  "(CASTERLEVEL*10) minutes", "(D)" -> "[D]", "(see text)" -> "; see text".
- SAVEINFO / SPELLRES capitalisation and "see text" notes normalised ("Yes (harmless)", "Will negates; see text").
- Personal-range spells get SAVEINFO:None and SPELLRES:No, as in the PHB.
- ITEM:Potion is added to spells that can be brewed into potions (lowest spell level 3rd or less, targets a
  creature, not personal or an area).
- Subschools and descriptors are in the right fields (the magazine sometimes wrote "Transmutation (Lawful)" or
  "[Teleportation]"), using the PHB's spellings (Mind-Affecting, Language-Dependent, See Text). "War" is a
  Dragon descriptor not used in the PHB and is kept.
- Fixed: "Area of Effect" target lines are now parsed (Pillar of Sand); "One day/level" durations; .MOD lines
  for spells whose keys contain commas are renamed with the key (Greater Bestow Curse, Mass Lesser Geas).
- Per-line SOURCELONG/SOURCESHORT/SOURCELINK are not repeated on each spell (the PHB file does this); the
  campaign-level source in the PCC covers it, and each DESC ends with the original magazine issue.
Verified in PCGen 6.08: no DMC load errors; every class and domain list contains exactly the expected DMC spells
(Wizard/Sorcerer 124, Cleric 70, Druid 45, Bard 31, Paladin 8, Ranger 6, Urban Druid 6, ...).

Ki feats (aligned with Complete Scoundrel / Complete Adventurer in the repo)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Deliberately kept separate:
- "any <type> feat" requirements (e.g. Arcane Focus Item: Craft Wondrous Item + any metamagic feat). PREABILITY counts matches,
  so PREABILITY:2,...,Craft Wondrous Item,TYPE.Metamagic would also pass with two metamagic feats and no Craft Wondrous Item.
  The repo keeps such requirements in separate tokens too.
- Chosen-skill bonuses (BONUS:SKILL|LIST|n): the repo never combines LIST with other skills (Dwarflore, Mercantile Background,
  Cultured Courtier).
- Alternatives ("one of these"), negated (!PRE...) and different-category tokens.
Verified in PCGen 6.08: Concealed Ambush - Hide 10 alone does not qualify, Hide 10 + Move Silently 10 does; Elflore still
gives +1 to Knowledge and Spellcraft. Loads without DMC errors.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
All situational feat and flaw bonuses: TEMPBONUS (consistent rule)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Every situational bonus or penalty in feats and flaws that affects the character's own (or, for auras, allies') saves,
skills, attacks, damage or AC is now a TEMPBONUS toggle with one TEMPDESC saying when to switch it on - 119 feats/flaws.
This follows the repo's TEMPBONUS syntax and logic: shown on PCGen's Temporary Bonus tab, counted only while switched on,
shown in the sheet's Temporary column; several TEMPBONUS tokens on one object share one TEMPDESC (like the repo's Rage);
TEMPBONUS may carry a prerequisite (Legendary Tracker's Survival bonus requires Track).
Bonus types: the type stated in the source is kept (dodge, shield, insight, morale, sacred, competence, inherent);
untyped situational bonuses are TYPE=Circumstance (142 repo TEMPBONUS tokens use that type; circumstance stacks in Bahamut35e).
This replaces the always-counted circumstance tags on the Birth and Ancestor feats (High/Rising Sun, Ally of the Ki-rin,
Forest Ambusher, Legendary Tracker, Mountain Hunter, Spellwise, Stalwart Defender).
Newly expressed: Girded Soul, Pureheart, Grave Friend, Green Heart, Hidden Thoughts, Lawful Discipline, Passive Reconnoiter,
Last Survivor, Friend of the Earth, Friend of Earth, High Society, Landless Nobility, Tested, Trap Mastery, Voice of the Mage,
Superior Taste/Touch, Twin Sword Style (shield), Stormheart (dodge), Ehlonna's Way (woodland skills), and the flaws Gullible,
Grudge Keeper, Warrior of the Phalanx, Bestial Instinct, Short Attention Span, Terrain Specialist, among others.
Permanent parts found on the way: Pureblooded Suel (+1 Intimidate), Footsteps of the Mage (+2 Balance), Voice of Winds (+2
Diplomacy/Intimidate when speaking - i.e. always).
Left as text: penalties on enemies (Fiendsign, Master of Mockery, hexblade curses), level/caster-level checks, Strength and
other ability checks, confirming criticals, option-based feats (Jinnbond, Tribal Trait, Trophy Hunter, Magical Insight),
cumulative or trade-off effects (Retribution, Precise Strike), and replacements of an existing bonus (Defensive Expert,
Tunnel Rat). Equipment keeps the repo's item convention (typed BONUS:SKILL, e.g. Hammer of the Weaponsmith).
Verified in PCGen 6.08: toggles off - Fortitude +5 (only Born Under a High Sun's permanent +1); switched on - Fortitude +10
(+1 fire, +4 disease, +2 illusions, -2 Gullible); Reflex/Will unchanged (+2 and -2 cancel). Loads without DMC errors.

Prerequisite audit: text prerequisites are never enforced by PCGen
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Reported: Extend Curse and Excised from the Web of Life were available to a Gruwaar rogue. PCGen never enforces PRETEXT, so any
feat whose requirement is only text is open to everyone. Fixes:
- Book-dependent checks now live in the base feats instead of book-only .MOD files: the hexblade curse
  (PREVARGTEQ:HexbladesCurseTimes,1), ninja ki / ki dodge / ghost step / ghost strike / sudden strike and scout skirmish
  (Complete Adventurer variables and abilities), the Oriental Adventures feats (Pain Touch, Ki Shout, ...), Extra Stunning.
  Without the book these variables/abilities don't exist, so nobody qualifies (verified: loads without errors either way).
  dmc_feats_oa.lst / dmc_feats_cw.lst are gone; dmc_feats_cadv.lst only keeps the scout bonus-feat list move.
- Specific spells/powers: PRESPELL (Excised from the Web of Life: PRESPELL:1,Blight; Betrayal of the Spirit Linked: Animate Dead;
  Matter Agitation, Control Flames, Control Body, Astral Construct).
- Repo forms for: wizard specialists (PRESUBCLASS), domains (PREDOMAIN), patron deity (PREDEITY), class skill (PRECSKILL),
  bardic knowledge / nature sense / poison use / mind blade / shape and throw mind blade / chastise spirits / spirit form /
  improved flanking / sense elements (PREABILITY), wild shape plant/elemental (DruidWildShapeLVL 12/16), eldritch blast
  (EldritchBlastDice), +N mind blade (soulknife level), alignments, races, "stat N or lower", "both arcane and divine",
  "inability to cast spells", Knowledge (any three) 6 ranks, and named feats from any repo book (Hold the Line, Thrall to
  Demon, Sacrificial Mastery). Harbinger feats require the Mooncalf race.
- Regions / Nations / Clans: like the repo's regional feats, PREREGION for places and peoples, and race/subrace checks for race
  entries (e.g. gray elf -> PREMULT:2,[PRERACE:1,Elf],[PREABILITY:1,CATEGORY=Internal,Elf ~ Gray]); "Nation: Any" has none.
- OCR fixes: "Can 15" -> Con 15, "Base attack bonus +G" -> +6 (inferred), "Dodge. Nimble Flier" -> two feats, "Drow Drow" -> Drow.
- Five more feats were missing because their type was misread ([PS10NIC], [P510NIC], [PSI0NIC], [METAPS1ONIC]): Align Mind Blade,
  Cerebremetamagic, Mystic Stability, Silver Mind Blade, Strategic Reassignment. Two-line names fixed: "Initiate" is
  Faerie Mysteries Initiate, "Linked" is Betrayal of the Spirit Linked.
Result: a 6th-level Gruwaar rogue qualifies for 85 compendium feats instead of 159. Text-only prerequisites remain only where
the repo has nothing to check (monster natural attacks, heat attack, energy resistance class ability, story requirements,
spells/powers with a given descriptor); 97 clauses in total, most of them story/campaign requirements.

Spell-descriptor / school prerequisites (repo forms PRESPELLDESCRIPTOR:<count>,<descriptor>=<min level> and PRESPELLSCHOOL):
- "Any two spells with the acid / electricity / cold / fire descriptor" -> PRESPELLDESCRIPTOR:2,Acid=0 etc. (Agitated Causticity,
  Bioelectrical Surge, Solid Freeze, Astral Fire); "ability to cast a spell with the fire (or light) descriptor" ->
  PRESPELLDESCRIPTOR:1,...; "at least three arcane divination spells" -> PRESPELLSCHOOL:3,Divination=0.
- Astral Fire's "two fire powers" and Harden Energy's "a force power + a spell with an energy descriptor" combine descriptor
  checks with a psionic manifester check (PCGen treats powers as spells, so the descriptor count can't tell them apart alone).
- Dual-Plane Summons: "any summon monster spell" -> PRESPELL:1,Summon Monster I,...,IX.
Verified in PCGen 6.08 (each prerequisite evaluated separately): a rogue fails both of Agitated Causticity's checks; a 4th-level
sorcerer knowing acid splash and acid arrow passes the acid-descriptor check but still fails the matter agitation power check.
Psi-spell feats and sacrifice feats:
- All nine Psi-Spell feats now require arcane spellcasting and psionic manifesting (PRESPELLTYPE:1,Arcane=0 and
  PREVARGTEQ:Caster_Level_Highest__Psionic,1), per the compendium: "psi-spell feats require both arcane spell slots and psionic
  power points". A cleric/psion therefore never qualifies, whatever powers or divine spells he has.
  Limitation: PRESPELLDESCRIPTOR can't be restricted to arcane spells, so a character who is ALSO an arcane caster could count
  divine spells of that descriptor.
- "Ability to prepare and cast <spell>" (Betrayal of the Spirit Linked, Excised from the Web of Life, Verminous Graft) now also
  requires a caster who prepares spells (PRESPELLCAST:MEMORIZE=Y, alongside the PRESPELL check).
- Betrayal of the Spirit Linked requires a familiar (its sacrifice is killing and animating your own familiar).
Verified in PCGen 6.08 (each prerequisite evaluated separately): cleric 5/psion 3 fails the arcane check; wizard 5/psion 3 passes

Purification feats ("ability to cast remove disease as a spell-like ability": Bane of Decay, Detoxifying Touch, Extra Remove
Disease, Smite Carrier, Strengthened Resilience, Wholesome Fare): this is now a spell-LIKE ability check instead of PRESPELL

Chaos Music (Perform 4 ranks + bardic music) and Darkness Familiar (a familiar) are real checks: a rogue fails them; a bard
(with the standard bard subclass) and a sorcerer with a familiar pass.
Bloodline feats re-tested on sorcerers (levels 1, 4, 9, 20): the bonus spells are added only once the sorcerer can cast that
spell level, and each raises the spells-known allowance by one at that level, so they are true bonus spells (sorcerer 9:

Vestiges compared with the repo's Tome of Magic vestiges
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Structure is identical to tm_abilities.lst / tm_spells.lst: an ability "<level> <Name>" (KEY:Vestige ~ <Name>, OUTPUTNAME:Bind
Vestige ~ <Name>, CATEGORY:Special Ability, TYPE:Vestige.SpecialQuality, PREVARGTEQ:BinderMaxVestigeLVL,<level>,
SPELLS:Bind Vestiges|TIMES=1|CASTERLEVEL=1|Bind Vestige ~ <Name>, COST:0, ASPECT:Binding DC|.., ASPECT:Special Requirement|..)
plus a "spell" (KEY:Bind Vestige ~ <Name>, TYPE:Vestige, RANGE:Personal, TARGETAREA:You, DURATION:24 hours, DESC lines for
sign, influence and granted abilities). Epic vestiges' binding DC is "--" because the compendium prints "—".
Added to match the repo's mechanical touches:
- Granted skill bonuses as BONUS:SITUATION|<skill>=Bound <vestige>|n (as ToM's Leraje and Paimon): Astaroth (both versions),
  Desharis, Kas, The Triad (Diplomacy, Sense Motive), Vanus. Not tagged: Astaroth's +4 on all Craft checks and the extra
  bonuses against evil outsiders / evil creatures, The Triad's Psicraft (Expanded Psionics skill), Primus's conditional +1.
- Scaling values in granted-ability descriptions use formulas as in ToM (e.g. Amon's breath): "DC 10 + 1/2 your effective
  binder level + your <ability> modifier" -> "DC %1|BinderEffectiveLVL/2+10+CHA", "caster level equals your effective binder
  level" -> %1|BinderEffectiveLVL, "N feet per effective binder level (maximum M feet)" -> min(N*BinderEffectiveLVL,M),
  "equal to your effective binder level" -> BinderEffectiveLVL (11 descriptions in 7 vestiges).
No vestige grants sneak attack (Kas and Gaia only mention it), so nothing like ToM's Malphas was needed.
Verified in PCGen 6.08: a 7th-level binder (Cha 18) sees Ahazu's Abduction "Will save (DC 17)" and blindsight "35 feet";
Kas's +4 Bluff registers as the situational bonus "Bound Kas". Loads without DMC errors.

MASTER (pp. 345-350) - built by master_build.py
- Class lines as the PHB Rogue: HD d6, PC.Base.NonCaster, BAB 3/4, good Will, poor Fort/Ref (with the alternate/fractional
  progression switches), 8 skill points. Class skills come from the chosen focus.
- Weapon and Armor Proficiency: simple weapons, light armor, shields except tower (PHB Cleric form).
- Primary Focus (1st): selection category "Master Primary Focus" - Craftsman, Performer, Professional or Sage; each grants its
  class skills (CSKILL) and a competence bonus on its key skill (Craft / Perform / Profession / Knowledge) of +2, +4 from 7th.
- Secondary Focus (7th): category "Master Secondary Focus" (cannot repeat the primary): adds its class skills and +2 competence.
- Knacks (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th, 13th, 16th, 19th): category "Master Knack" (like the PHB Rogue's special abilities); each knack is
  only available with the matching primary or secondary focus, plus its own prerequisites (skill ranks, earlier knacks,

  repo's Dragonlance _feats.lst that defines them is not loaded by any data set.

1 secondary, 4 knacks, 3 bonus feats, 5 Skill Mastery; Appraise and Spellcraft class skills, Tumble not; Craft 6 ranks = 12

======================================================================
FILE LAYOUT (consolidated)
======================================================================
48 files reduced to 33 data files + the PCC. Files of the same type and the same load condition are merged (a PCC line loads a
whole file under one condition, so files with different conditions or object types must stay separate). Inside merged files,
"###Block:" comments mark the original sections.
- dmc_abilitycategories.lst  <- feat/racial categories, class categories (Master), Method wizard-feat categories
- dmc_abilities.lst          <- racial traits, class features (Master), domain powers, Method internal abilities, spell templates
- dmc_feats.lst              <- all feats incl. support feats (Sword & Fist, Races of Krynn)
- dmc_equip.lst              (user-maintained goods file, unchanged), dmc_equip_power_components.lst (generated to spec)
- dmc_equip_weapons_armor.lst <- weapons + armor;  dmc_equip_magic.lst <- wondrous items, magic weapons, magic armor
- dmc_equipmods.lst          <- equipment modifiers + magic special abilities
- one file per optional book where content depends on it: _xph (Expanded Psionics: racial, domain, feat additions), _sc,
  _bovd, _cadv, _ca, _rs, _rw, vestiges (Tome of Magic), flaws (Unearthed Arcana), feats_nocw (only without Complete Warrior)
- separate by type as in the repo: races, templates, languages, domains, spells + class/domain spell lists (like the PHB's
  ph_spells / ph_spells_class), weapon and armor proficiencies, classes.
Build: the generators write to a working folder; the final step (consolidate.py) assembles this layout and rewrites the PCC,
checking that every data line appears exactly once (3,290 lines before and after) and that merged files share one load condition.
Verified after consolidation: loads without DMC errors (with/without optional books) and all earlier test results unchanged.

dmc_equip.lst is GENERATED again (no longer a hand-maintained copy). The user's edits are built into the generator
(equip_build.py + equip_user_changes.json) and re-applied on every build:
- power components are not in this file (they are generated to spec in dmc_equip_power_components.lst);
- the four grimoires use the user's descriptions (the grimoire's own text, its language and +2 circumstance bonus,
  "Suggested Class: Wizard", reference) instead of the shared "wizard library" introduction; "Dimensions of Evil: A Guidebook
  to the Nether Realms" is named with a dash (KEY kept as in the user's file); its description no longer repeats the subtitle;
- Lichbriar (seedling): the lichbriar steals 1000 XP (was 10); Salamander Orchid (mature): 500 gp and 1000 XP (was 100).
Verified: the generated file matches the user's upload item for item (294 items, same order); the only differences are the
agreed bonus tags (28 BONUS:SKILL, 2 TEMPBONUS/TEMPDESC, BONUS:SITUATION replaced on 19 items).
Consolidation only takes files that the build's PCC loads; anything else left in the working folder is reported and ignored,
so a file a generator stops writing can never linger in the data set.
"Any other (divine) spellcasting class level 1st": Holy Mount -> PRECLASS:2,SPELLCASTER.Divine=1 (the paladin, whose special mount
